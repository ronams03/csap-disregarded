About Us

The City Scholars' Association in PHINMA Cagayan de Oro College (CSAP) is a school-based organization that is responsible for overseeing scholars' welfare and development in PHINMA-COC.

With the tagline “To Serve and Guide the Oro Scholars in PHINMA-COC,” the organization aims to lead and equip the Iskolar Sa Dakbayan with the necessary skills and knowledge through engaging in different social initiatives that are anchored on four ladderized formations of the City Education Development Office — Leading the self; Leading Others; Leading the Community; and Leading the nation.

Mission

To strengthen the Scholar's participation in community development through a series of social action and formation activities and initiatives.

To uphold academic excellence and to create an inclusive environment for everyone.

Vision

To become a school-based organization that enables every City Scholar to seize and appreciate open opportunities for individual betterment.

To fulfill this purpose, CSAP-COC endeavors to make a meaningful difference in the community through our succeeding programs.

The Legacy of CSAP-COC: Our History

Building a Foundation for City Scholars — Est. 2019

The story of the City Scholars Association in PHINMA COC (CSAP-COC) began with a mission to create a dedicated support system for the scholar community.

2019: The Foundation

In 2019, Mr. Liam Aiken R. Osorio recognized the need for a unified voice and a structured organization to oversee the welfare of city scholars within PHINMA-Cagayan de Oro College. As the Founding President, he established the framework that continues to define our association today.

Our Purpose from Day One

From its inception, CSAP-COC was built to be more than just a school-based organization. It was designed as a pillar of support, focusing on:

Scholar AdvocacyEnsuring the needs and voices of city scholars are heard.
Holistic DevelopmentCreating opportunities for growth beyond the classroom.
Community WelfareBuilding a “home” where every scholar feels valued and protected.

A Growing Heritage

What started as a vision by Mr. Osorio has evolved into a vital part of the PHINMA-COC student experience. Since 2019, we have remained committed to the development and success of every scholar who joins our family.

All About City Scholarship

The Cagayan de Oro City Scholarship Program is a government-funded initiative that provides comprehensive financial aid, including full tuition and monthly stipends, to eligible Kagay-anon students. Managed by the City Scholarships Office, it offers various tracks for academic overachievers, low-income families, and even students pursuing law or medical degrees.

Scholar Development Program (SDP)

The Scholar Development Program (SDP) consists of activities that City Scholars are required to complete every semester. These activities may be conducted within the school or in the community/barangay, and are designed to promote leadership, service, and personal development among scholars.

Types of Scholar Development Program (SDP)

1. Community Volunteerism

Week-long or month-long activities usually implemented by community-based organizations that address community needs and promote civic engagement.

2. Ladderized Activities

Implemented by the City Education Office (CEDO) to develop scholars’ leadership and character formation. Examples:

  • KATIG
  • ISLA Modules
  • School-based & Community-based General Assembly
  • FIOGET

3. Institutionalized Volunteerism

One-shot or short-term volunteer activities within the school, community, or through CEDO. Examples:

  • Klarex na Kalimpyo
  • CEDO Calls for Volunteers
  • School-based & Community-based initiated activities

Frequently Asked Questions

SDP — How many SDPs are we going to render per semester?
7 SDP per semester.
GC — How to join my College Group chat?
Message the college director and representative from your respective department and send them your CSR as proof that you are a city scholar grantee.
Scholarship — Why are we a City Scholar and HK Scholar at the same time?
There is an agreement between the CEDO and PHINMA COC that once you are a city scholar and you enroll at COC, you will automatically have two (2) scholarships: the Internal Scholarship (HK 50%) and the External Scholarship (City Scholar 50%).
Scholarship — Do we have to render both City Scholar and HK Scholar duties?
Yes, we have to render both City Scholar and HK duties. Failure to do so will result in the untagging of the scholarship.
Scholarship — When do we renew our scholarship?
Both HK and City scholarships should be renewed every after the semester.
Scholarship — I haven’t met the required GWA, does that mean I am no longer a city scholar?
You will be called by the CEDO and will be marked as on-probation. If your grades don’t improve and still don’t meet the required GWA, that is the time you will be removed as a city scholar.
Scholarship — If I lose my City Scholarship, will my HK Scholarship still remain?
Yes. You still have your HK Scholarship, since it doesn’t have a grade requirement.
Scholarship — If I haven’t renewed my City Scholarship from the CEDO, will my scholarship be removed?
Yes, because it is stated in the contract that we are required to renew our scholarship and render our SDP every semester.

All About Hawak-Kamay Scholarship

The Hawak Kamay (HK) Scholarship is a financial aid program offered by PHINMA Education in the Philippines. It is designed to make college affordable for students from low-income families.

Fixed Low TuitionA consistent, discounted rate of ₱5,500 to ₱12,500 per semester (rates vary by school/program).
No Entrance ExamsAdmission is based on your determination to learn, not a high-pressure test.
No Maintaining GradeAs long as you pass your subjects, you keep your scholarship.
Guaranteed CoverageThe discount applies from your first year until you graduate.

Who Can Apply?

  • Incoming First-Year College Students.
  • Transferees (depending on the specific PHINMA campus policy).
  • Students with a strong desire to finish their degree despite financial challenges.

Benefits of HK Scholarship

HK5.5₱5,500 per semester
HK7.5as low as ₱7,500 per semester
HK10as low as ₱10,000 per semester
HK12.5as low as ₱12,000 per semester
Note: Books and uniforms are not included.

Eligibility & Requirements

Eligibility

  • The student must be an incoming freshman.
  • The student must belong to a low-income family.
  • The student must pass the scholarship exam.

Requirements

Submit the following documents at the Marketing Department, on or before the set deadline:

  • Form 137
  • Certificate of Good Moral Character
  • NSO Birth Certificate
Maintenance: The scholarship extends for the normal life of the chosen program upon enrollment in first year. Must be able to render 80 hours/semester.

Exam Permit and Guidelines

  • Log in to your SIS account and download your exam permits.
  • If you weren’t able to download your permit, you need to pay first your balance and go to assessment for approval.
  • If you don’t have any balance, just go directly to the assessment for approval — just state that you are a city scholar.

HK Scholarship Duty

  • After enrollment, the CSDL Office will email you regarding your duty assignment and its schedule.
  • If you weren’t able to accomplish your duty within the assigned schedule, you can directly go to the CSDL Office and look for Ma’am April Rose V. Selim so she can give you another duty assignment.
  • City Scholars are required to render 25 hours of duty to the department/office assigned to you.

HK Scholarship Renewal

The renewal form and steps will be posted through PHINMA COC City Scholar and PHINMA COC Center for Student Development and Leadership.

Requirements needed for renewal

  • Freshmen — School ID, HK Orientation, and HK LEADS.
  • Upperclassmen — DTR signed by the department/office assigned to you.
  • Fourth Year — School ID only.
  • Working Student — Submit Certificate of Employment (COE).
NOTE: Failure to renew the HK Scholarship means the scholarship will be untagged, and you will have to pay the remaining 50% of your tuition.

Awards and Recognition

Kinanao Awardee Through the Years

The Kinanao Awards is an annual recognition program in Cagayan de Oro City dedicated to honoring the exceptional achievements of city scholars and their organizations. Organized by the City Scholarships Office (CSO), the name is derived from the Higaonon term kinanao, meaning “learner.”

Rather than focusing solely on grades, the awards celebrate scholars who demonstrate a holistic commitment to leadership, community service, and volunteerism. It serves as a prestigious platform to spotlight individuals and groups who embody the spirit of being “scholars for the people,” encouraging the city’s youth to balance academic excellence with active civic engagement for the development of Northern Mindanao.
